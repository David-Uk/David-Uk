🚀 What I do
I'm an independent product-focused builder based in Lagos, Nigeria, working across the full stack — product strategy,
architecture, and implementation. I like taking an idea from a blank page to a working platform, then hardening it
with the security instincts of a cybersecurity analyst and the leverage of AI tooling.
🏗️ Product & platform engineering — multi-tenant SaaS, marketplaces, and operational tools for SMEs
🤖 AI engineering — LLM-powered features, content generation, and intelligent automation
🔐 Cybersecurity-minded development — building with secure defaults, not bolting security on after
🌍 Focused on solving problems for the Nigerian and broader African market, with platforms built to scale globally
